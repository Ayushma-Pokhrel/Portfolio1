About Me

I am a passionate physics student. My deep interest in physics goes beyond textbooks and examinations—I genuinely enjoy understanding, solving, and explaining physics at its core.

What excites me most about physics is the way simple principles lead to powerful results through logical reasoning and mathematical derivations. I love working through physics problems step by step, uncovering the ideas behind formulas rather than memorizing them. For me, derivations are not just calculations; they are stories that explain how nature works.

Alongside learning, I am deeply interested in teaching physics concepts in a clear, intuitive, and student-friendly way. I believe that physics becomes truly beautiful when complex ideas are broken down into simple reasoning, visual thinking, and real-world connections. Whether it is classical mechanics, electromagnetism, or mathematical methods in physics, I enjoy guiding others through concepts with clarity and patience.

My long-term goal is to grow as a physicist and an educator, contributing to physics learning by making it accessible, rigorous, and conceptually clear. If you are curious about physics or want to strengthen your understanding, you are in the right place
